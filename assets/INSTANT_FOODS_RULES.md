# CarbCalc UA — Instant foods calculation rules

Category: Продукти швидкого приготування

## Dry basis
When nutrition is stated for the packaged dry product, store and calculate carbohydrates from the dry product weight. Mark the record as dry and keep nutrition per 100 g of dry product plus the exact dry package or serving weight when available.

Adding water increases prepared weight but does not add carbohydrates. Therefore nutrition per 100 g dry product must not be treated as nutrition per 100 g prepared product.

For a single-serving pack, total carbohydrates from the dry pack remain the same after adding only water.

## Prepared weight
Calculation from the weight of the prepared hydrated food is allowed only when the actual dry amount and final prepared yield are known, or when the manufacturer provides verified nutrition specifically for the prepared product.

Do not invent a water absorption or preparation yield coefficient.

## Label basis
During audit, explicitly determine whether manufacturer nutrition refers to dry product or prepared product. Conflicting or unclear basis stays pending_review and outside runtime.
